GScholar is an Android app for people who read research papers on their phone. It is my most-used piece of software: **100,000+ downloads on Google Play with a 4.4 star rating** (October 2026). I designed, built and monetised it, and I still maintain it.

## What it does

- **Search and read**: a fast WebView client for scholarly search, with an in-app PDF viewer, downloads and bookmarks.
- **Chat with a paper**: ask questions about the paper you are reading, including plain-language ("explain like I'm five") explanations.
- **Compare papers**: put several papers side by side and get a structured comparison of their methods and findings.
- **Citations and notes**: a citation generator and per-paper notes.
- **Habits**: reading streaks and a dashboard of what you have read.

## Engineering

- Flutter and Dart, shipped and updated through the Play Console.
- LLM summaries are cached per paper, so each one is generated once rather than on every visit.
- Revenue from AdMob (native, interstitial, app-open and rewarded formats behind one shared cooldown, so ads never stack) plus a Pro subscription with a free trial.

## What it shows

It shows the full product loop: building the app, getting it through Play review, growing it to six figures of installs, and earning from it without driving users away.
